STEP 01

Welcome to SalesNova

SalesNova is a smart sales management and forecasting system designed to help businesses organize their sales, monitor inventory, understand performance and plan ahead.

STEP 02

Create your account

Start by creating your SalesNova account. Provide your name, business name, email address and password to create your personal business workspace.

STEP 03

Add your products

Add the products your business sells, including their prices and available stock. This gives SalesNova the information it needs to help you monitor your inventory.

STEP 04

Record your sales

Record each transaction as it happens. SalesNova uses your sales records to update your business statistics and create useful performance insights.

STEP 05

Understand your business

Your dashboard transforms your recorded data into useful information such as total sales, transactions, product performance, sales trends and inventory insights.

STEP 06

Forecast and plan ahead

SalesNova uses your recorded sales history and recent sales patterns to estimate future sales and provide useful recommendations that can support better business planning and decision-making.
